# 画像計画

料理を伝えるための生成画像。実際の商品写真ではなく、公開前に店舗による内容確認、または許諾済み実写への差し替えが必要。WebPをローカル保存。元画像は image-sources.json、サイズは image-inventory.json を参照。

## hero.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural, Yamagata soba and ramen restaurant landing-page food image. Warm natural daylight on dark honey wood table, simple indigo-and-white Japanese ceramic bowl, authentic everyday portions. Landscape 3:2, entire dish with safe margins. No people, hands, chopsticks, text, logos, watermark, invented interior or exterior. One large ordinary Japanese donburi bowl of hot tori chuka. Distinct thin wavy yellow ramen noodles in clear soy-dashi broth, cooked chicken pieces, generous thinly sliced leek, thin strips of burdock root and carrot. Natural steam. No pork, no boiled egg, no nori. Whole bowl in frame.
